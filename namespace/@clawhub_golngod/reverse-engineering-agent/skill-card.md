## Description: <br>
技术逆向分析Agent：快速看透任何产品/平台/系统的技术本质--它怎么做的、为什么这么做、是不是真的在做；覆盖SaaS、AI Agent、移动App、开源项目、API服务等。 <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Developers, product teams, founders, and technical diligence reviewers use this skill to analyze observable evidence about digital products, competitors, APIs, agent systems, and open-source projects. It produces evidence-based technical assessment reports that separate facts from inferences and support practical decisions. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill is broad and dual-use because reverse-engineering analysis can be applied to products, APIs, and agent systems. <br>
Mitigation: Use it only on systems the user is authorized to assess and limit analysis to public, lawful, observable evidence. <br>
Risk: Architecture, authentication, database, or prompt conclusions may be inferred from partial observations. <br>
Mitigation: Treat those conclusions as provisional unless supported by clear evidence, and require explicit fact/inference/confidence labeling in reports. <br>
